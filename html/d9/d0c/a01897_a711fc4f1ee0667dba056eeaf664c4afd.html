enum SystemEx.Numeric.BigDecimal.RoundMode

Specifies the rounding strategy used by BigDecimal.Round(int, RoundMode). These modes correspond to common numerical rounding conventions used in scientific, financial, and engineering computations.

Enumerator
ToEven 

Round to the nearest value; if equidistant, round to the nearest even number. Also known as “banker's rounding”.

AwayFromZero 

Round away from zero, increasing magnitude regardless of sign.

TowardZero 

Round toward zero, decreasing magnitude regardless of sign.

Floor 

Round toward negative infinity. Equivalent to mathematical floor.

Ceiling 

Round toward positive infinity. Equivalent to mathematical ceiling.
