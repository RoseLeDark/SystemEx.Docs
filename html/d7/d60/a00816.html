◆ SystemEx::Numeric::Uint256

struct SystemEx::Numeric::Uint256

Represents a 256‑bit unsigned integer composed of two 128‑bit halves.

Uint256 is a structural container used by high‑precision floating‑point formats and extended numeric systems. It stores the lower and upper 128‑bit segments explicitly, enabling deterministic bit‑level operations, custom floating‑point encodings, and arbitrary‑precision arithmetic.

The type does not implement arithmetic itself; it is intended as a raw storage primitive for IBigFloat<TSelf> and other extended numeric abstractions.
