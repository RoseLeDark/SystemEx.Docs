Detailed Description

Enumerates the bits of a BitUIntSpan according to its indexing mode.

Warning: When the span is in FlexSpanMode.Ring, enumeration is infinite. Consumers must implement their own termination conditions to avoid non-returning loops.

The enumerator yields live bit values. Mutating the underlying integer during enumeration will affect subsequent results.
