◆ SIUnit

enum SystemEx.Numeric.SIUnit

Defines the set of supported SI engineering units for formatting BigDecimal values. These units correspond to standard physical quantities used throughout scientific, electrical, mechanical, and general technical disciplines.

Enumerator
Ohm 

Ohm (Ω). The SI unit of electrical resistance, used extensively in electronics, circuit design, signal processing, and power systems.

Ampere 

Ampere (A). The SI base unit of electric current, fundamental in electrical engineering, electromagnetism, and power distribution.

Volt 

Volt (V). The SI unit of electric potential and electromotive force, used in circuit analysis, power systems, and electronic device design.

Farad 

Farad (F). The SI unit of capacitance, widely used in electronics, signal filtering, energy storage, and RF engineering.

Henry 

Henry (H). The SI unit of inductance, used in electromagnetics, transformer design, power electronics, and RF systems.

Meter 

Meter (m). The SI base unit of length, used across physics, engineering, construction, manufacturing, and scientific measurement.

Gram 

Gram (g). The SI unit of mass (derived from kilogram), used in chemistry, material science, laboratory measurement, and industrial processes.

Joule 

Joule (J). The SI unit of energy, used in thermodynamics, mechanics, electrical systems, and general physics.

Watt 

Watt (W). The SI unit of power, representing energy per unit time. Used in electrical engineering, mechanics, HVAC, and energy systems.

Hertz 

Hertz (Hz). The SI unit of frequency, used in signal processing, communications, acoustics, control systems, and physics.
