enum SystemEx.Utils.CompareResult : sbyte

Specifies the result of a comparison between two values.

This enumeration is used by CompFunc<T> and the generic algorithms in Algorithm to express ordering relations between two operands A and B. It generalizes the usual "less/greater/equal" semantics with additional states for "equal but smaller" and "equal but larger" to support nuanced ordering logic.

Enumerator
Less 

Alias for CompareResult.AIsSmallerB. Indicates that the first operand is strictly smaller than the second.

Greater 

Alias for CompareResult.AIsLargerB. Indicates that the first operand is strictly larger than the second.

EqualLess 

Alias for CompareResult.AIsEqualSmallerB. Indicates that the first operand is equal to the second but considered "smaller" in a secondary ordering dimension.

EqualGreater 

Alias for CompareResult.AIsEqualLargerB. Indicates that the first operand is equal to the second but considered "larger" in a secondary ordering dimension.

AIsLargerB 

The first operand A is strictly larger than the second operand B.

AIsSmallerB 

The first operand A is strictly smaller than the second operand B.

Equal 

The operands A and B are considered equal in the primary ordering dimension.

AIsEqualLargerB 

The operands are equal in the primary dimension, but A is treated as "greater" in a secondary dimension (e.g. tie‑breaking).

AIsEqualSmallerB 

The operands are equal in the primary dimension, but A is treated as "smaller" in a secondary dimension.

Null 

One or both operands are null, or the comparison function cannot produce a meaningful ordering result.
